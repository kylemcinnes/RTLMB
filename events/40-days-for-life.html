40 Days for Life (Mississauga)

Help save lives. Join a peaceful, prayerful vigil to end abortion—40 days of prayer & fasting, community outreach, and a loving presence outside abortion facilities. The Fall 2026 campaign runs Wednesday, September 23 – Sunday, November 1, 2026.

Mid Point Mass

Invitation to the Mid Point Mass ...we are halfway through the 2026 campaign

Dear supporters of the 40 Days for Life campaign in Mississauga

We’re already about a quarter of a way through the 40 days campaign. And with this passing of time, the Midpoint Mass is coming up.

It will be a time of celebration, encouragement and the regrouping of our purpose. Please join us whether you have prayed on site during this fall vigil or not. We hope you can make it. Please spread the word!

This is a gentle reminder that the ongoing 40 Days prayer vigil for life ends November 1st. There are still plenty of empty slots left to fill so please go to www.40daysforlife.com/mississauga to sign up. All that is required is for people to go to the site to pray. No witness other than our presence is expected. Please sign up and encourage others to do so!

  • Mid Point Mass: Sunday, October 18 at 1:00 pm
  • St. Catherine of Siena Parish, 2340 Hurontario St.

Fall 2026 Campaign

Wednesday, September 23 – Sunday, November 1, 2026

You can protect mothers and children by joining this worldwide mobilization. Through prayer and fasting, peaceful vigils, and community outreach, 40 Days for Life has inspired over one million volunteers.

  • Daily vigil hours: 8:00 am – 8:00 pm
  • 40 consecutive days of prayer & fasting
  • Peaceful, lawful, public witness

Mississauga Vigil Location

The peaceful and prayerful vigil is held near the Women’s Clinic at 101 The Queensway—southwest corner of Confederation Parkway & Queensway (50 steps west of Confederation Parkway on the south side), by the candy-cane-shaped vent stack and metal hand rail.

Bubble-zone compliant directions:

  • Park at St. Catherine of Siena Catholic Church, 2340 Hurontario St.
  • Walk south on Hurontario, then west on Queensway toward the hospital
  • Pray at the new vigil site on the south sidewalk/pathway (see map below)

All vigils follow Ontario’s safe-access zone laws—please follow campaign guidelines and coordinator instructions.
